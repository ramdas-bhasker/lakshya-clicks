01 — Gentle, Baby-First Sessions No Rush • No Harsh Flash • Right At Home

Your Baby Sets The Pace.
We Simply Follow Along.

“New父母 often worry: what if the baby cries or needs to feed during the shoot? Our answer is always the same—we pause, we wait, and we never watch the clock.”

In those first few weeks of life, your newborn’s comfort and safety come before everything else. We bring sanitized organic cotton wraps, soft natural lighting (never harsh camera flashes), and years of gentle experience right to your home—or welcome you into our warm, quiet studio.

Alongside newborn portraits (ideally between days 7 and 21), we love photographing traditional Indian family rituals like your baby’s Namakaran / Barasala cradle ceremony, Annaprashan (first rice ceremony), Mundan, and 6-to-12-month sitting-up milestones.

Unhurried 3-Hour Newborn Session
