01 — Life's Biggest Milestones Family Traditions & Corporate Excellence

Be Present With Your Guests.
Leave The Memories To Us.

“When you’re hosting a celebration, you’re busy welcoming guests and making sure everyone has eaten—our job is to make sure you don’t miss a single smile.”

In Indian families, some of our most cherished gatherings happen outside of weddings. Whether it’s your baby’s First Birthday, a daughter’s traditional Half-Saree / Langa Voni / Manjal Neerattu Vizha (Puberty) ceremony, a Gruhapravesam housewarming, or your parents’ 25th and 50th Wedding Anniversary, we bring the same editorial care and warmth to every family event.

We also partner with leading companies and founders across India to document Corporate Events, leadership summits, product launches, and annual award galas—delivering same-day press & LinkedIn highlights alongside full multi-camera coverage.

01

1st Birthdays & Cake Smash

We arrive early to photograph your custom stage decor and pre-party baby portraits before guests arrive, followed by full candid coverage of the cake-cutting and family fun.

02

Puberty & Half-Saree Ceremonies

Graceful portraits in traditional Langa Voni and Kanjeevaram silk sarees, floral mandap decor, maternal uncle (Mama) seer varisai processions, and family blessings.

03

25th & 50th Anniversaries

Celebrate your parents’ or grandparents’ journey with family portraits across three generations, varmala renewal, speeches, and a heartfelt anniversary film.

04

Corporate Conferences & Galas

Professional coverage of keynote speakers, panel discussions, brand activations, award nights, and executive headshots with 24-hour PR photo turnaround.

05

Gruhapravesam & Sacred Pujas

Respectful documentation of housewarming ceremonies, thread ceremonies (Upanayanam), and home pujas with elders and relatives.

06

Highlight Reels & Event Albums
