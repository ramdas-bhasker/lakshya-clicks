01 — Celebrating Motherhood Baby Shower (Seemantham / Godh Bharai) & Portraits

Warm, Unhurried & Completely
Built Around Your Comfort.

“There is nothing quite like the warmth of an Indian baby shower—the sound of glass bangles, the scent of fresh jasmine gajra, and three generations of women blessing the mother-to-be.”

Whether you’re hosting a traditional Seemantham, Godh Bharai, or Valaikappu ceremony with your extended family or looking for a quiet, intimate outdoor or home maternity portrait session with your partner, we make sure the entire experience feels effortless and comfortable.

We take plenty of breaks whenever you need to sit down or sip water, guide you gently into flattering, natural postures, and capture both the joyful family rituals and the quiet connection between the two of you before your baby arrives.

What’s Included In Our Baby Shower & Maternity Sessions

01

Seemantham & Godh Bharai Rituals

Full candid and traditional coverage of your baby shower ceremony—capturing the glass-bangle ceremony, kumkum blessings, aarti, and joyful moments with parents and in-laws.

02

Traditional Silk Saree Portraits

Editorial portraits celebrating your Kanjeevaram, Paithani, or Banarasi saree, temple jewelry, and jasmine flowers in soft, flattering light.

03

Couple & Older Sibling Frames

We include heartwarming portraits with your husband, older children, and even family pets so everyone is part of welcoming the new baby.

04

Home, Studio Or Outdoor Garden

Choose between the comfort of your own home, our climate-controlled studio, or a quiet morning garden with plenty of seating and rest breaks.

05

Highlight Reel & Ceremony Film

Optional 4K baby shower highlight film capturing the songs, blessings, and laughter of your family so your child can watch it when they grow up.

06

Fine-Art Keepsake Album

High-resolution online gallery delivered within 10 days, plus handcrafted linen albums that pair seamlessly with your upcoming newborn session.
