01 — The Heart Of An Indian Wedding Every Ritual Has A Reason

We Don't Just Photograph Events.
We Photograph Relationships.

“When you open your wedding album ten years from now, you should be able to hear your cousins cheering during the varmala and feel your mother’s hand on your shoulder.”

An Indian wedding moves fast. One moment you’re having turmeric applied by your favorite aunties at the Haldi, and the next you’re walking under a floral phoolon ki chadar toward the mandap. In the middle of all that excitement, there are hundreds of tiny moments you don’t even get to see while you’re on stage.

That’s why we’re there. While our editorial portrait team creates timeless frames of your bridal lehenga, sherwani, and heirloom jewelry in just 15–20 relaxed minutes, our candid and traditional photographers have their eyes on your parents, grandparents, and best friends—capturing the whole story of your wedding from every angle.

02 — Complete Wedding Coverage Everything Your Family Needs

What’s Included In Our Wedding Collections

Whether you’re hosting an intimate morning muhurtham at home or a three-day destination celebration at a palace resort, we tailor our crew to your schedule.

01

Bridal Shringaar & Groom Prep

Quiet, unhurried portraits as you get ready—capturing your handwoven lehenga or Kanjeevaram silk, heirloom kundan jewelry, safa tying, and the first look with your parents.

02

Haldi, Mehendi & Sangeet

Complete candid and traditional coverage of your pre-wedding festivities—from flying marigold petals and brass urli water splashes to high-energy Sangeet dance performances.

03

Baraat, Varmala & Sacred Pheras

Multi-angle coverage of the groom’s baraat, the bridal entry, varmala petal showers, Kanyadaan, Hastmelap, Jeelakarra Bellam, mangalsutra, sindoor, and pheras around the sacred fire.

04

Warm Family & Elder Portraits

Dedicated traditional photographers who patiently organize and photograph every family group, grandparent, and relative with warmth and respect.

05

5-Day Sneak Peek & Online Gallery

Receive 40–50 edited highlight photographs within 5 to 7 days of your wedding, followed by your complete high-resolution online gallery for easy sharing with family worldwide.

06

Handcrafted Fine-Art Albums

Archival lay-flat wedding albums bound in Italian linen and vegan leather, plus two companion parent albums designed in-house with your favorite photographs.
