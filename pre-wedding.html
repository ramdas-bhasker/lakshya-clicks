01 — Why Couples Love Outdoor Shoots No Stiff Poses • Just The Two Of You

Think Of It As An Evening Date
Where We Happen To Bring A Camera.

“Almost every couple starts their outdoor shoot telling us they don’t know how to pose. Ten minutes later, they’re laughing like they’ve known us for years.”

During your wedding days, you’re surrounded by hundreds of guests, relatives, and back-to-back rituals. An Outdoor or Pre-Wedding shoot gives the two of you a breather—a chance to wear outfits you love, walk through a heritage fort, a lush garden, or a quiet beach at sunset, and just enjoy being engaged.

Beyond giving you beautiful photographs and save-the-date reels for your invitations and Sangeet LED screens, our outdoor session is the best way for you to get comfortable in front of our cameras before the wedding day arrives.

02 — The Outdoor Experience Everything Thought Through For You

What’s Included In Our Outdoor & Pre-Wedding Sessions

01

Location & Concept Scouting

Whether you love royal stepwells and forts in Rajasthan, coastal beaches in Goa and Gokarna, or lush green courtyards closer to home, we help pick the perfect spot and time of day.

02

Outfit & Color Palette Guidance

We guide you on 2 to 3 outfit changes—pairing flowing sarees, ethnic kurtas, or contemporary gowns with the colors of the landscape so your portraits pop naturally.

03

Relaxed, Unposed Direction

No cheesy props or awkward stances. We use simple movement prompts and conversation so your smiles and chemistry look 100% genuine.

04

Golden Hour & Twilight Portraits

We shoot through sunrise or sunset into twilight, using soft off-camera lighting after dusk for dramatic evening portraits.

05

Save-The-Date Cinema Reel

Add a 60–90 second 4K Save-The-Date film with drone visuals and music—perfect for WhatsApp wedding invitations and Sangeet screens.

06

75+ Color-Graded High-Res Photos

Delivered in a private online gallery within 10 days, ready for large-format framing, signing boards, and guestbook albums.
