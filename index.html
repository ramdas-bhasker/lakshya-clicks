06 — Everything You Need To Know Frequently Asked Questions

Questions Couples & Families
Often Ask Us.

Because we never treat your wedding or family celebration like just another date on a calendar. Over the last decade, we’ve photographed more than 450 Indian weddings, engagements, and milestone celebrations—and we know the most meaningful photographs happen when you feel completely relaxed around us. We blend in like family, anticipate the quiet emotional glances between your parents, and craft timeless photographs and films that feel 100% like you.
We strike a natural balance between unscripted candid storytelling and editorial grace. Instead of pulling you away from your guests for hours of stiff, repetitive posing, we let your celebrations unfold organically while guiding you gently during portraits. Our color grading is warm, true-to-life, and timeless—honoring the rich crimson silks, haldi yellows, and heirloom gold of Indian traditions without heavy filters that look dated a few years later.
Yes, absolutely! While our studio is based in India, more than half of the weddings we document take us to destinations across Udaipur, Jaipur, Goa, Mahabalipuram, Coorg, Kerala, Mussoorie, Lonavala, and international venues. Whether you’re planning a royal palace wedding or an intimate beachside varmala, our team travels wherever your story takes us.
Our team size is tailored to your guest count, number of events, and venue scale. For an intimate celebration, engagement, or baby shower (100–200 guests), a core team of 3 to 4 specialists (candid photographer, cinematographer, and traditional photographer/filmmaker) is ideal. For a grand multi-day Indian wedding (300–1000+ guests), we typically bring a crew of 6 to 8 senior artists—including aerial drone pilots and dedicated lighting assistants—so not a single moment is missed.
Every celebration is unique—some families need photography for a single-day muhurtham, birthday, or puberty ceremony, while others host a three-day wedding with Haldi, Mehendi, Sangeet, Wedding, and Reception. Once you share your event dates, schedule, and approximate guest count with us, we put together a transparent, customized quote within 24 hours with zero hidden charges.
We know how excited you and your family are to relive every moment! We share a curated Sneak Peek Gallery of 40–50 edited high-resolution photographs within 5 to 7 days of your celebration. Your complete, color-graded online photo gallery is delivered within 4 to 6 weeks, and your cinematic wedding teaser, full feature film, and handcrafted fine-art albums are delivered within 8 to 10 weeks.
