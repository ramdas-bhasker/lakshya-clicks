04 — Our Process From Our First Chai To Your Family Heirloom

How We Work With You

Step 01

A Conversation Over Chai

We sit down in person or on a video call to hear how the two of you met, what rituals mean the most to your parents, and the vibe you want for your celebration.

Step 02

Light & Timeline Planning

We coordinate directly with your family or wedding planner so golden-hour portraits, Haldi timing, and stage lighting look effortless without delaying your rituals.

Step 03

Unobtrusive Storytelling

On the days of your celebration, our photographers and filmmakers arrive early, dress for the occasion, and capture every laugh, tear, and dance move with zero intrusion.

Step 04

Handcrafted Films & Albums

Every photograph is individually color-graded, and your wedding film and Italian archival albums are handcrafted to stay in your family for generations.
